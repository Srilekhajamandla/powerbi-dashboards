# 👟 Adidas Sales Report Dashboard | Power BI

This project presents a fully interactive **Adidas Sales Report Dashboard** built in Power BI.  
It provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance, operating profit, units sold, product trends, retailer-wise contribution, and region-wise insights.

This dashboard helps business users understand revenue drivers, product performance, and regional sales distribution in a visually rich and actionable format.

---

## 🚀 Key Insights Delivered

### ✔️ Overall KPIs
- **Total Sales:** $822M  
- **Operating Profit:** $303M  
- **Units Sold:** 2M  
- **Price per Unit:** $44  
- **Operating Margin:** 42%

### ✔️ Sales Trends
- Month-wise sales trend analysis
- Highest performing month identified (July–August range)
- Seasonal patterns in customer purchases

### ✔️ Region-Level Insights
- Sales distribution across West, Northeast, Southeast, South, and Midwest
- Region-wise percentage contribution to total revenue

### ✔️ Product-Level Performance
- Top-selling product lines including:
  - Men’s Street Footwear  
  - Women’s Apparel  
  - Women’s Street Footwear  
  - Men’s Apparel  
  - Women’s Athletic Footwear  
- Horizontal bar charts for clear comparison

### ✔️ Retailer Contribution
- Retailer-wise sales performance:
  - West Gear  
  - Foot Locker  
  - Sports Direct  
  - Kohl’s  
  - Walmart  
  - Amazon  
- Contribution stack from lowest to highest

3. Use filters for:
   - Region  
   - Invoice Date  
4. Explore retailer performance, product contribution, and monthly sales trends
